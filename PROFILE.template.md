# Profile — <your name>

Fill this in and keep it in your workspace root. Skills read it to calibrate. Without it they
produce competent, generic work; with it they produce work that sounds like you and stops at
the right level of finish.

Be specific. "High quality" tells an agent nothing. "Two typos is fine in an internal Slack
message and unacceptable in a client proposal" tells it everything.
